How it works

Every website starts closed. To open one: breathe, write why, and pick how long (1–30 min). Links you click on an open site open too, on its timer. The site opens in black & white with a ticking clock, then closes again when the time is up. Several sites can be open at once, each with its own timer.

Always open

These sites never ask (subdomains included: python.org also opens docs.python.org).

😵‍💫 Distracting sites

Two extra rules for these: opening one more than 3 times in an hour adds one more set of breaths each time, and 🔬 research mode (15–60 min of free links, started from the popup) doesn't open them — they still ask why. Every other site always gets the normal breaths.

Before a site opens

Distracting sites opened often add more (see above). Searches from the address bar skip the breathing.
